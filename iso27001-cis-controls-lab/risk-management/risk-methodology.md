# Risk Assessment Methodology
**Document Type:** Risk Management Methodology  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Owner:** ISMS Risk Manager  
**Last Updated:** 2026-05-18  
**Classification:** Internal

---

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the standardized methodology for calculating and evaluating information security risk levels within the organization.

---

## 2. Risk Formula

Risk is calculated as:

> **Risk = Likelihood × Impact**

---

## 3. Likelihood Scale

| Score | Description |
|------|-------------|
| 1 | Rare |
| 2 | Unlikely |
| 3 | Possible |
| 4 | Likely |
| 5 | Almost Certain |

---

## 4. Impact Scale

| Score | Description |
|------|-------------|
| 1 | Negligible |
| 2 | Minor |
| 3 | Moderate |
| 4 | Major |
| 5 | Critical |

---

## 5. Risk Rating Matrix

| Impact \ Likelihood | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
|---------------------|---|---|---|---|---|
| 5 Critical | M | H | H | C | C |
| 4 Major    | M | M | H | H | C |
| 3 Moderate | L | M | M | H | H |
| 2 Minor    | L | L | M | M | H |
| 1 Negligible | L | L | L | M | M |

**Legend:**
- L = Low
- M = Medium
- H = High
- C = Critical

---

## 6. Risk Acceptance Criteria

- Low: Acceptable
- Medium: Management approval required
- High: Treatment mandatory
- Critical: Immediate action required
