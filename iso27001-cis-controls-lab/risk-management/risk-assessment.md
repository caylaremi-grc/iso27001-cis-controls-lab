# Risk Assessment 
**Document Type:** Risk Management Policy  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Owner:** ISMS Risk Manager  
**Last Updated:** 2026-05-18  
**Classification:** Internal

---

## 1. Purpose

This Risk Assessment Policy defines the process for identifying, analyzing, and evaluating information security risks to organizational assets.

It ensures risks are assessed in a consistent and repeatable manner in alignment with **ISO/IEC 27001:2022** requirements.

---

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all:
- Information systems
- Data assets
- Cloud services
- Third-party vendors
- Business processes handling sensitive data

---

## 3. Risk Assessment Principles

- Risks are identified based on threats and vulnerabilities
- Impact is evaluated against confidentiality, integrity, and availability (CIA)
- Likelihood is assessed based on current controls and exposure
- Risk assessments must be documented and reviewed regularly

---

## 4. Risk Assessment Frequency

Risk assessments must be performed:
- At least annually
- Before onboarding new systems or vendors
- After significant incidents or system changes

---

## 5. Roles & Responsibilities

- **ISMS Lead:** Oversees risk governance
- **Asset Owners:** Identify and report risks
- **IT/Security Teams:** Provide technical input
- **Management:** Accept or reject risk treatment decisions

---

## 6. Compliance Mapping

- ISO/IEC 27001: Clause 6.1.2 Risk Assessment
- ISO/IEC 27005 Risk Management Guidance
