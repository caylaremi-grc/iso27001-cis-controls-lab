## 1. Purpose

This Risk Treatment Plan defines how identified risks are addressed through mitigation, transfer, avoidance, or acceptance.

---

## 2. Risk Treatment Options

- **Mitigate:** Implement controls to reduce risk
- **Transfer:** Outsource or insure risk
- **Avoid:** Eliminate the risk source
- **Accept:** Accept residual risk with approval

Residual risks must be:
- Documented
- Approved by management
- Reviewed annually
