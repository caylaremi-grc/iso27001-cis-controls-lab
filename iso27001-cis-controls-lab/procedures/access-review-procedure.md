# Access Review Procedure

## Purpose
Ensure user access rights remain appropriate and aligned with business requirements.

## Procedure
1. Export current user access lists
2. Review privileged and high-risk accounts
3. Validate business justification with managers
4. Remove unnecessary access
5. Document approvals and actions
6. Retain audit evidence

## Frequency
- Quarterly for critical systems
- Annually for standard systems

## Evidence
- Access review reports
- Approval records
- Ticket references
