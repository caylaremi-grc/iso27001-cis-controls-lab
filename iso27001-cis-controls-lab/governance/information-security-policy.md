# Information Security Policy

## Purpose
The purpose of this policy is to establish the organization's commitment to protecting information assets.

## Scope
This policy applies to all employees, contractors, and third parties.

## Roles and Responsibilities
- Management: Approve and support security initiatives
- IT Team: Implement technical controls
- Employees: Follow security requirements

## Policy Statements
- Information assets must be protected according to classification.
- Access must follow least privilege principles.
- Security incidents must be reported immediately.
- MFA must be enabled for critical systems.

## Enforcement
Violations may result in disciplinary action.

## Review Cycle
This policy shall be reviewed annually.
