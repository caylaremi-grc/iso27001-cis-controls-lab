# Acceptable Use Policy (AUP)
**Document Type:** Governance Policy  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Owner:** Information Security / ISMS Lead  
**Last Updated:** 2026-05-18  
**Classification:** Internal

---

## 1. Purpose

This Acceptable Use Policy (AUP) defines the rules for appropriate use of organizational information systems, data, and technology resources.  

The purpose is to protect the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of systems and data in alignment with **ISO/IEC 27001** requirements and applicable regulatory obligations (including **GDPR/FADP** where relevant).

---

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to:
- Employees
- Contractors
- Third-party users
- Vendors with system access

It covers all organizational assets including:
- End-user devices
- Cloud services
- Email and collaboration tools
- Healthcare and business systems (e.g., EHR, lab systems)
- Networks and storage systems

---

## 3. General Acceptable Use

Users must:
- Use systems only for authorized business purposes
- Follow the principle of least privilege
- Protect confidential and sensitive information
- Lock devices when unattended
- Report security incidents or suspicious activity immediately

Users must not:
- Share accounts or credentials
- Bypass security controls (MFA, access restrictions, logging)
- Install unauthorized software or tools
- Access or attempt to access data without authorization

---

## 4. Data Protection Requirements

All users must comply with:
- Data classification rules (Public / Internal / Confidential / Highly Confidential)
- Encryption requirements for sensitive data in transit and at rest
- Secure handling of personal and medical data
- Data minimization and retention policies

Special protection is required for:
- Patient medical records (EHR systems)
- Financial and billing data
- Employee personal data

---

## 5. Access Control Rules

- Access is granted based on business need and role (RBAC)
- Privileged access must be approved and logged
- Shared accounts are strictly prohibited
- Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) is required for critical systems

Relevant controls:
- **ISO/IEC 27001:** A.5.15 Access Control, A.8.2 Privileged Access
- **CIS Controls v8:** 5 Account Management, 6 Access Control Management

---

## 6. Email, Communication & Internet Use

Users must:
- Use corporate communication tools for business purposes only
- Avoid clicking suspicious links or attachments
- Refrain from sending sensitive data outside approved channels

Prohibited activities include:
- Phishing, spam, or malicious communication
- Use of unauthorized messaging applications for sensitive data

---

## 7. Device & Software Usage

- Only approved devices may access corporate systems
- Antivirus and endpoint protection must remain enabled
- Security patches must be installed promptly
- Unauthorized software installation is prohibited

---

## 8. Cloud & Third-Party Services

- Only approved cloud services may be used
- Data must not be uploaded to unauthorized SaaS tools
- Third-party access must be formally approved and monitored

Relevant controls:
- **ISO/IEC 27001:** A.5.19 Supplier Relationships, A.5.23 Cloud Services
- **CIS Controls v8:** 15 Service Provider Management

---

## 9. Monitoring & Logging

The organization may monitor:
- System access
- Network activity
- Security logs
- Email and system usage (where legally permitted)

Monitoring is performed to ensure:
- Security compliance
- Incident detection and response
- Audit and regulatory requirements

---

## 10. Security Incident Reporting

Users must immediately report:
- Lost or stolen devices
- Suspicious emails or activity
- Unauthorized access attempts
- Data breaches or leaks

Reports must be made to:
- IT Security Team / SOC
- ISMS Incident Response Channel

---

## 11. Enforcement

Violations of this policy may result in:
- Access removal
- Disciplinary action
- Contract termination
- Legal action where applicable

---

## 12. Compliance References

This policy aligns with:
- ISO/IEC 27001: Information Security Management System (ISMS)
- ISO/IEC 27002: Security Controls Implementation Guidance
- CIS Controls v8
- GDPR / Swiss FADP (where applicable)

---

## 13. Review Cycle

This policy is reviewed:
- Annually, or
- After major security incidents or system changes
